⚙ HOW TO PLAY
🎮 THE GOAL
Keep the ball bouncing. Destroy all the bricks to level up. Don't let the ball fall off the bottom!
↔️ PADDLE CONTROL
Move your paddle left and right to bounce the ball. Use mouse, keyboard arrows, or swipe on mobile.
⚡ THREE WEAPONS
⚡
GRAVITY QUAKETaps phone or G key. Creates a shockwave that damages random bricks.
◈
NOVA BLASTHold Space or button, then release. Fires upward to destroy a full row of bricks.
⌖
PHOTON RAYDouble-tap or L key. Rapid-fire lasers for 10 seconds. Auto-recharges after use.
📊 TOP BAR (HUD)
Score: Your points. Bigger combos = bigger bonuses. Level: Increases when you clear all bricks. Music gets faster! Combo: Chain brick hits to multiply your score. Lives: You start with 3. Lose them all and it's game over. Power: Shows ability charge status.
🧱 BRICKS
Normal Bricks: 1 hit destroys them. Shielded Bricks: Have a glowing shield—takes 2 hits to break (shield breaks first, then brick). Shields don't break during DROP events, only the brick takes damage. Stronger bricks = more points!
🔥 THE DROP
Every 16 bars of music, a DROP event triggers (warning pulse + 12-second window). During DROP: ×5 damage to bricks and ×5 score multiplier! Watch for the red visual cues and plan your shots to maximize destruction during this window.
✨ GOOD BONUSES
💜 MAGNET: Ball sticks to paddle. Aim and release for precision shots.
⭐ MULTI: Ball splits into multiple balls for massive destruction!
🛡️ SHIELD: Protects from losing one life—ball bounces back up once.
↔️ WIDE: Paddle becomes wider for easier catching—lasts 14 seconds.
⧖ SLOW: Slows down the ball for easier control.
▲ LASER: Activates rapid-fire laser cannons (same as L key ability).
⏱️ WARP: Temporarily speeds up the paddle movement.
💥 BOOM: Ball becomes explosive—destroys surrounding bricks on impact.
❌ BAD PENALTIES
↕ NARROW: Paddle shrinks temporarily—makes catching the ball harder. Avoid these!
G GHOST: Ball becomes invisible for a moment—harder to track.
⌨️ QUICK CONTROLS
P or ESC: Pause/Resume the game anytime.
